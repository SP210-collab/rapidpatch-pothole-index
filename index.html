About the Rapidpatch Pothole Index

Why this exists

Auckland's potholes are invisible until you hit one. Then they own your Wednesday — bent wheels, blown tyres, suspension claims, insurance hassle.

Our crews place a numbered flag in every pothole we're about to fix. Each flag is sequential (#001, #002…), geotagged, and tracked on this page. It's the only public, live record of where Auckland is breaking and how fast it's being repaired.

How the scheme works

Property owner spots a hole → photo-quotes via rapidpatch.co.nz → fixed-price quote in 2 hours → crew dispatched → flag planted → repair within 5–7 working days → 12-month warranty.

Data

Counts on this page reflect flags placed by Rapidpatch crews on private property (driveways, body corp carparks, retirement village internal roads, commercial yards) with the owner's consent. Council and Kāinga Ora work is included only where we've been engaged. Auckland Transport response data sourced separately via LGOIMA (reference CAS-PR0R8-Y6J2N5).
